Who We Are

FLUVION INFRATECH PRIVATE LIMITED

FLUVION INFRATECH PRIVATE LIMITED is an India-based company focused on engineering, procurement and construction (EPC) solutions for water, wastewater, industrial effluent and environmental infrastructure.

Based in Uttarakhand, the company aims to support government departments, public-sector undertakings (PSUs), private industries, project owners and EPC partners through project-oriented solutions tailored to technical requirements and agreed scopes.

🌊

Cleaner Water. Greener Tomorrow.

Our focus spans water treatment, wastewater management, industrial effluent, recycling, desalination, equipment integration and related infrastructure.
